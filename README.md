# NUDGE demo (v0.1)

Demo of NUDGE inside a throwaway WooCommerce shop. Nothing is installed on your computer, no login, no real customers.

**Open (1 click):** https://playground.wordpress.net/?blueprint-url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SUPERMAGIC1111/nudge-demo-playground/main/blueprint.json

Then "NUDGE (demo)" opens. Build an email, click "Send test email to me" (goes only to the admin of the throwaway shop), and see it under Tools > WP Mail Logging.

Made-up customer data. Prototype v0.1 by AIgotya. Feedback welcome.

---

**Español:** demo de NUDGE dentro de una tienda WooCommerce de prueba que corre en tu navegador. Sin instalar nada, sin login, sin clientes reales. Abre el enlace de arriba, crea un email en "NUDGE (demo)" y pulsa "Send test email to me" (solo se envía al administrador de la tienda de prueba). Míralo en Herramientas > WP Mail Logging.
